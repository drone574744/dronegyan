Padhna shuru karein

Drone kya hota hai?

Drone ek aisa vimaan hai jisme pilot andar nahi baithta. Use door se remote ya mobile app se chalaya jata hai, ya wo pehle se tay raste par apne aap udta hai. Isko UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) bhi kehte hain.

Zyadatar drone me motor aur propeller hote hain jo hawa ko neeche dhakelte hain, isse drone upar uthta hai. Chaar motor wale drone ko quadcopter kehte hain.

Drone ke types

Multi-rotor

Sabse common. Ek jagah hawa me ruk sakta hai. Photography aur chhote kaam ke liye achha.

Fixed-wing

Hawai jahaz jaisa. Lambi doori aur der tak udta hai. Mapping aur survey me use hota hai.

Single-rotor

Helicopter jaisa. Bhaari saaman utha sakta hai, lekin mehnga hota hai.

Hybrid VTOL

Seedha upar uthta hai aur phir jahaz ki tarah udta hai. Dono ke fayde milte hain.

Drone ke mukhya parts

Frame

Drone ka dhancha jisme sab parts jude hote hain.

Motors aur propellers

Drone ko uthane ki takat dete hain.

ESC

Har motor ki speed ko control karta hai.

Flight controller

Drone ka dimaag. Sensor padh kar use sthir rakhta hai.

Battery (LiPo)

Poore drone ko bijli deti hai.

GPS aur camera

Jagah pata karne aur photo ya video lene ke liye.

Drone kahan use hote hain?

  • Shaadi aur film ki photography
  • Kheti me dawa ka chhidkaav aur fasal ki nigrani
  • Zameen ka survey aur nakshe banana
  • Bijli ke taar aur tower ki jaanch
  • Baadh ya aag me bachav aur khoj
  • Saaman ki delivery

India me drone ke niyam (sankshep me)

  • Drone Rules 2021 ke tahat drone udane ke niyam tay hain.
  • Digital Sky platform par zone dekhe jaate hain: Green, Yellow aur Red.
  • Airport ke paas aur sensitive jagah (Red zone) me bina anumati udana mana hai.
  • Kuch weight ke upar ke drone ke liye registration ya license chahiye.

Niyam badalte rehte hain. Udane se pehle hamesha sarkari Digital Sky website par taaza jankari dekh lein.

Aksar puchhe jaane wale sawal

Beginner ke liye kaunsa drone theek hai?

Chhota multi-rotor drone jisme GPS aur auto-return ho, shuru karne ke liye surakshit rehta hai.

Drone kitni oonchai tak ud sakta hai?

India ke niyam me aam taur par zameen se 120 meter tak ki seema hoti hai. Pakki jankari ke liye sarkari niyam dekhein.

Kya drone ko mobile se chala sakte hain?

Haan, zyadatar drone ki app hoti hai jo mobile se jud kar camera aur control deti hai.
